# TAMVER Research v0.8 — Multilingual Foundation

Languages: English (root), Russian /ru, Spanish /es, Catalan /ca, French /fr, German /de, Italian /it, Portuguese /pt.

## Implemented
- Language selector in main menu.
- Localized header, menu, footer, office dialog.
- Localized home, Knowledge, Methodologies, Research, Evidence, Impact, Ledger and Andorra page shells.
- Localized Decision Crystal labels and links.
- Localized Andorra Scenario Lab control labels.
- `hreflang` alternate links.
- Deep knowledge article routes exist for all languages. Their body currently falls back to English with a visible draft notice until terminology review.

## Translation policy
DSS, AIR, SCM, DSI, DCI and product/system names remain stable acronyms. Source cassette data, scenario names, evidence excerpts and methodological quotations are not silently machine-translated; they remain English until separately reviewed.
