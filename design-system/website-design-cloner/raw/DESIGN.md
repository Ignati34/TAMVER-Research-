## Observed visual grammar
1. Large uppercase **TAMVER** wordmark with unusually wide letter spacing.
2. Thin/light sans-serif editorial typography and uppercase section titles.
3. Near-black and very dark olive photographic surfaces.
4. Alternation with neutral off-white editorial sections.
5. Hairline borders and geometric overlays; very low corner radius.
6. Warm amber/gold illumination used as a premium energy accent rather than a general UI color.
7. Large negative space; content blocks are not dense SaaS cards.
8. A cinematic globe section uses luminous warm lines and nodes over a dark sphere.
9. Cooperation area uses three light cards with editorial text above monochrome/architectural photography.
10. Footer uses a restrained geographic line: MADRID / VIENNA / ANDORRA.
